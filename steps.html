Tano POC · 9 steps

From a hashtag to a sale

How the POC finds Instagram creators, understands them, and runs a brand campaign with them. One example runs through every step: the brand Petalwork and the creator Studio Vega.

  • Each step writes only its own tables. It can read the others, but never changes them.
  • Steps pull their work. No step sends work to another. Each one checks the database for something new to do.
Part 1

Collect creator data

Find creators, download them, understand them, make them searchable.

  1. 1

    Discover · find names

    Look at hashtags and mentions to find new creators. At this point we only keep the name.

    #ceramics → @studio.vega. Later, her post mentions @lino.home, so he is found too.
    Writes discovery_runsdiscovery_candidatessocial_accounts
  2. 2

    Ingest · download the creator

    Get the profile and the posts. Post text and numbers go in the database. Photos and videos go in S3, and the database keeps their location.

    Studio Vega: 42k followers, Lisbon. 1 Reel = 1 row in content, 1 row in media, 1 video file in S3.
    Writes creatorsaccount_profilescontentmediaS3 files
  3. 3

    Enrich · understand the content

    Read the caption, the video and the bio, and write down facts. Every fact keeps its proof.

    "These vases are from Petalwork… Made in Lisbon" → works with Petalwork (0.95), lives in Lisbon (0.9).
    Writes extraction_runsentitiesentity_aliasesfactsevidence
  4. 4

    Index · make it searchable

    Put each creator, with their posts and facts, into the search engine. Only what changed gets updated.

    Studio Vega becomes one search document in OpenSearch.
    Writes index_sync_stateOpenSearch
  5. 5

    Search · answer questions

    Find creators by words and filters.

    "home decor in Lisbon" → Studio Vega
    Writes nothing
Part 2

Run a brand campaign

Petalwork's campaign "Autumn at home", built on the data from part 1.

  1. 6

    Set up · the brand writes the plan

    Goal, budget, gifts and rules.

    €20k in sales, at most €35 spent per sale, a free vase as the gift. No creators who work with competitors. 5k–200k followers, in Portugal, Spain or France.
    Writes brandsproductsprogrammesguardrails
  2. 7

    Find creators · choose the best

    Search, check the rules against the facts, give each creator a score, and keep the best.

    Studio Vega ✓ 0.88 · Casa Nord ✗ Denmark and works with a competitor
    Writes programme_candidates
  3. 8

    Get them posting · contact and gift

    Email the creator. If they say yes, send the gift with a discount code and a link.

    Studio Vega says yes and gets a vase and the code VEGA15. Lino Home says no.
    Writes outreach_threadsoutreach_messagesgiftscreator_codes
  4. 9

    Learn · measure the result

    Find the creator's post and count the sales that used their code or link.

    2 sales (€38.25 + €71). The vase cost €24, so €12 per sale ✓ under the €35 target.
    Writes programme_postssales
